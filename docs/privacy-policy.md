# Pai Pai Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

Pai Pai ("the app") helps shopkeepers track inventory, sales and customer credit (udhaar). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

## Data stored on your device

Everything you enter — your shop profile, items, stock, sales, customers and credit ledger — is stored locally on your phone in an on-device database. It is not shared with anyone unless you use Backup (below).

## Contacts

If you tap "pick from contacts" when adding a customer, the app asks for permission to read your contacts. It only reads the name and phone number of the contact you choose and saves them as a customer in the app. Your contact list is never uploaded, and the app never changes your contacts. You can deny this permission and type customer details by hand.

## Backup and restore

If you add an email address in your profile, the app backs up your data to our server once a day when you open it, and whenever you tap "Backup now". A backup contains your profile (including email), items, customers (names and phone numbers), sales, credit ledger and stock history. It is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and is used only to restore your data, for example when you change phones.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for analytics or tracking.

## Deleting your data

- **On your device:** use Settings → Danger Zone, or uninstall the app.
- **Backups on our server:** email us at the address below from the email used for backup, and we will delete them within 30 days.

## Children

The app is meant for business use and is not directed at children under 13.
